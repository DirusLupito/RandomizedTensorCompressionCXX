Experiments rely upon the NetCDF version of various files 
found at https://cgl.ethz.ch/research/visualization/data.php. These can be found under:
Cylinder Flow (Synthetic): https://cgl.ethz.ch/Downloads/Data/ScientificData/jungtelziemniak2d_nc.zip
Heated Cylinder with Boussinesq Approximation: https://cgl.ethz.ch/Downloads/Data/ScientificData/boussinesq2d_nc.zip
Cylinder Flow Around Corners: https://cgl.ethz.ch/Downloads/Data/ScientificData/pipedcylinder2d_nc.zip
Research Vessel Tangaroa: https://cgl.ethz.ch/Downloads/Data/ScientificData/tangaroa3d_nc.zip

The following files are assumed to be in .mat format, and may require some manipulation on your part to get them into the proper format
The sea surface temperature dataset comes from https://psl.noaa.gov/repository/entry/show?entryid=12159560-ab82-48a1-b3e4-88ace20475cd. It is truncated from 1727 down to 200 frames.
The cylinderFlowKarmanTSVDM2Experiment script uses a truncated version of the Cylinder Flow with von Karman Vortex Street data found at https://cgl.ethz.ch/research/visualization/data.php. It is truncated from 1501 down to 200 frames.
CYLINDER_ALL.mat: http://dmdbook.com/. It's the CYLINDER_ALL.mat file inside of the DATA.zip file.
